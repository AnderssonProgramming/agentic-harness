# Your first week

**Day 1:** get the app running locally (README, "Quick start") with the mock engine. Read `CLAUDE.md`: it's the contract every change follows, for people and for the coding agent.

**Day 2:** read `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Decision index, then two ADRs that interest you. Each ADR says why we chose something and what we rejected.

**Day 3:** pick a backlog item marked `good first item` with your buddy. Write the plan in `docs/plans/` and show it before writing code.

**Days 4–5:** build it one step per commit, with evidence for each acceptance criterion, and open your first pull request.

Who to ask:

- **Ana** (Product Owner): what to build, priorities, and anything about users.
- **Your buddy:** setup problems, the codebase, and reviews.
- **Compass:** anything you'd hesitate to ask a person. No question is too basic.

There are no stupid questions in your first month. Asking early is cheaper than guessing.
